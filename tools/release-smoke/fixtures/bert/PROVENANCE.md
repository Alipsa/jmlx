# Tiny BERT smoke tokenizer

Hand-written WordPiece vocabulary for the synthetic 32-token BERT checkpoint,
not taken from an external model. IDs 0–7 are inside its vocabulary.
The two smoke texts encode to [2,4,5,3] and [2,6,7,3]; unknown words map to 1.
