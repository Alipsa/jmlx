jmlx-native-macos-arm64 bundles precompiled binaries built from:

- MLX (https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx) -- existing MIT grant for Apple/MLX contributions,
  Copyright (c) 2023 Apple Inc.; separately identified third-party material retains its terms.
- mlx-c (https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-c) -- MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 ml-explore.
- metal-cpp (Apple's header-only Metal C++ interface, vendored inside MLX and compiled into
  libmlx.dylib) -- Apache License 2.0, Copyright Apple Inc.

See META-INF/LICENSE for the MIT grant covering the jmlx native artifact code,
META-INF/LICENSE-APACHE-2.0 for the complete Apache License 2.0 text, and
META-INF/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES for the retained MLX MIT grant and other upstream terms.
META-INF/LICENSE-CC-BY-SA-4.0 contains the complete CC BY-SA 4.0 text for Juffa's covered
expression in the reviewed Metal erf and Metal/CPU SIMD erfinv material. Its scope and
redistribution basis are identified in the Norbert Juffa notice section. No CC adapter's
license is identified for Apple/MLX contributions.
META-INF/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES carries the additional MLX/JACCL compiled-in dependency texts,
including JSON, fmt, gguflib/FP16, pocketfft, V8, ThreadPool, NumPy/opt_einsum,
supervised-lda, SIMD math, PyTorch, TensorFlow and GPU/PRNG algorithm references.
MLX adaptations remain modifications of the attributed upstream code; the listed reference
versions are license-evidence snapshots where MLX does not pin an upstream revision.
The release audit and unresolved provenance findings are recorded in
req/phase8-4-native-license-audit.md in the jmlx source repository.
